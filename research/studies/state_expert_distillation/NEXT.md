# Decisions after the fixed pilot

The completed pilot failed both prediction and external criteria. Preserve the spatial architecture, qualified feature conversion and exact recovery as reusable research components, but do not spend a larger training budget on this configuration. A 20.35% value-MSE reduction coexisted with a 24.46% policy-KL regression and substantially more unresolved games. The independent audit found no move, score, artifact or process-integrity failure that would explain those results away.

The immediate learning question is expert-target and endgame quality, rather than another unchecked increase in model size. Inspect policy/value behavior on the actually visited capped states and separate it from aggregate held-out error. Determine whether the teacher mixture, missing local tactical/scoring information, or loss/optimization balance explains the policy regression. These are hypotheses, not findings. Any score/ownership intervention needs a fresh controlled protocol; earlier small score-utility and ownership studies were negative and remain so.

Restore a coherent online self-play control before more offline architecture iterations. A bounded extension of an existing qualified Gumbel learner should preserve complete optimizer, replay, actor and RNG state through an explicit immutable checkpoint fork, with its new training schedule declared. Qualify the fork against a same-source continuation before a longer run. Compare real sample/compute learning curves and absolute KataGo results. No such fork or extended training has been launched by this study.

For a future promising representation, compare both equal-example and equal-compute budgets. Spatial attention processes every pre-action board, whereas the history model amortizes work over a sequence. The present 4,096-update comparison does not establish compute efficiency. Preserve initialization, teacher provenance, exposure counts, timings, caps and qualification costs. Report an independent seed separately rather than treating additional openings from one trained pair as model replication. D4 symmetry, exact legality features, pooling and removal of the history trunk are bundled here; augmentation and component ablations would be separate hypotheses.

Any later online test of the spatial expert needs the exact pending-leaf feature contract used in this study, an owned replay schema, complete optimizer/actor/sampler recovery and a contemporaneous search/model control. The failed pilot does not prioritize that port over the existing qualified learner. A cheap offline imitator is useful only if it supports improved self-play learning or better strength for a fixed inference budget. Keep real external anchors, expand to 19x19 only after an absolute 9x9 improvement, and qualify a current KataGo checkpoint/backend before making claims about current KataGo. These historical anchors remain useful for continuity but cannot answer that final question.

## Relation to paired multi-step decoding

The two objectives remain distinct. The expert learns search-improved policy/value targets; the observer predicts actual opponent actions from history, including a weaker opponent's style. The observer is not the expert prior on opponent MCTS nodes. This pilot holds the existing observer fixed and trains only the expert, so it cannot claim improved opponent adaptation.

A spatial expert can be combined with a causal observer in a fused proposal graph: advance a cheap speculative board representation, alternate expert and observer proposals for 2N plies, and batch k independent traces. The existing place-only transition is a conservative starting point. Rust must verify every model input before consuming a proposal. For this new expert that includes exact legality and pass/turn/komi context, as well as stones and action history. The old board-only trace validation is insufficient for the new feature contract. Captures, ko and an incorrect legality prediction trigger fallback before an affected policy row is used.

This does not remove the need to establish search semantics. Agreement of forecast actions or feature tensors does not prove that unchanged MCTS would choose those actions. First measure exact fixed-policy rollouts at matched total work, with the sequential model as the control and actual accepted depth, bytes, CPU time and device traces recorded. Then test using proposals to populate native search caches, preserving complete MCTS decisions/targets at the same search budget. An approximation that changes planning is a separate learning experiment with paired external evaluation.

Native workers should own request tickets, validation metadata, queue slots and tree arenas. Keep per-worker metadata on separate cache lines and publish batches rather than atomically updating every node through a shared queue. Retain validated KV once per game, using short branch deltas. This follows the measured dense-cache failure and partial compact-cache gain; it is not yet an implemented throughput improvement. The important measure is complete searched self-play progress per reserved chip-hour, alongside CPU use and device counters sufficient to support any MFU claim.

Production still requires independent actor/inference/learner job groups, storage outside the pod, host-loss and network-partition recovery, retention and an explicitly bounded sustained schedule. Same-disk checkpoint continuation and good scores on a small panel do not qualify those pieces.
